   * Verify clarity via the "One-Sentence Test" (must describe campaign goal in one sentence).

   * Apply "Text Message Test" (ensure email reads like a personal message from a friend).

   * Formulate hypothesis and send test batches (500–1,000 sends).

   * Optimize future copy variants based on performance data rather than intuition.
