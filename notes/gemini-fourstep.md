### 4. THE BUILD ORDER

1.  **Define the 4-Step Email Structure:**
    *   **Part 1: Personalization:** Maximum 1–2 sentences using informal greeting and cold-reading observation.
    *   **Part 2: Who Am I:** Social proof statement linking current success with similar clients.
    *   **Part 3: Offer:** Specific, quantified, time-bound result combined with full risk reversal.
    *   **Part 4: CTA:** Direct ask proposing two concrete times for a 15-minute call.
2.  **Define the Offer Formula:**
    $$\text{CVR \%} = \frac{\text{ROI} \times \text{Trust}}{\text{Friction}}$$
    *   *ROI:* Quantified, clear, time-bound result.
    *   *Trust:* Built via rapport, social proof, in-group positioning, and authority.
    *   *Friction:* Extent of client effort and upfront financial risk.
3.  **Construct Outreach Templates:** Draft email templates matching the 4-step framework and risk-reversal formula.

---

### 5. GOTCHAS

*   **Avoid LLM Personalization Slop:** Flattery about generic corporate metrics (e.g., "love your passion for process optimization") instantly signals automated spam.
*   **Keep Personalization Brief:** Limit personalization to 1 or 2 sentences max; longer intros decrease reply rates.
*   **Never Use Vague CTAs:** Avoid phrases like "let me know your thoughts." Every back-and-forth email leaks ~5% of your funnel conversion rate.
*   **Strategic Typos:** Intentionally leaving a minor typo near the end of a template (e.g., "gve" instead of "give") increases perceived human authenticity.

*   Configuration of cold outreach sending software, SMTP warmups, or deliverability settings.
*   Handling prospect replies or booking friction on the actual sales call.
